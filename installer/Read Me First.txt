KINTSUGI  金継ぎ
a Reluctant Astronaut plugin, restored by OBP

HOW TO INSTALL (Mac)

1. Double-click "Kintsugi.pkg".

2. macOS will probably stop you with a message that it "cannot verify" the
   installer or that it's from an "unidentified developer". That's only because
   this plugin is made by a friend, not bought from a company that pays Apple
   to sign its software. To open it anyway:

     - Click "Done" (or "OK") to close the message.
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down. You'll see a line about "Kintsugi.pkg" with an
       "Open Anyway" button. Click it, and confirm with your password.

   (On older macOS versions you can instead right-click Kintsugi.pkg,
   choose Open, and click Open again.)

3. Follow the installer: Continue, Install, and your Mac password.
   It installs both versions: VST3 (Cubase, Ableton Live, Reaper, Studio One...)
   and Audio Unit (Logic Pro, GarageBand).

4. Open your music program and let it find the plugin:
     - Cubase: Studio > VST Plug-in Manager > Rescan.
       Kintsugi is under Distortion, from Reluctant Astronaut.
     - Logic / GarageBand: found automatically when you open it.
     - Others: restart, or use the program's plugin rescan.

GETTING STARTED

Put Kintsugi on every track, and one more on the master bus with Glue turned
up. The defaults are deliberately subtle: you'll feel it more than hear it.

TO UNINSTALL

Delete these two items (Finder > Go > Go to Folder... and paste the path):
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Kintsugi.vst3
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Kintsugi.component
