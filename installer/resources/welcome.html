KINTSUGI

金継ぎ  ·  a Reluctant Astronaut plugin, restored by OBP

Kintsugi is the Japanese art of mending broken pottery with gold. The cracks aren't hidden; they become the most beautiful part.

This plugin brings that idea to sound: the warmth of a console, a valve and a reel-to-reel machine, and a slow, living instability. Subtle by default. Made to make a track feel alive, filled with feeling and imperfect beauty.

The installer puts the plugin in the standard place on your Mac, where every music program looks for it. You'll be asked for your Mac password, because plugins are installed for all users.

Installs to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or later.
