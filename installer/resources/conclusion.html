Kintsugi is installed.

Next: open your music program and let it find the new plugin.

  • Cubase: Studio → VST Plug-in Manager → Rescan. Kintsugi appears under Distortion, from Reluctant Astronaut.
  • Logic Pro / GarageBand: just open it; new plugins are found automatically at start-up.
  • Ableton Live, Reaper, Studio One and others: restart the program, or use its plugin rescan.

Getting started: put Kintsugi on every track, and one more on the master with Glue turned up. The defaults are deliberately subtle: you'll feel it more than hear it.
